1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the PowerBreeze public website. The system will present HVAC and appliance services, service areas, company information, customer testimonials, and online inquiry options.

1.2 Scope

PowerBreeze is a responsive business website for residential and commercial customers. It will allow customers to learn about available services, submit quote requests, and contact the business by telephone or email.

The in-scope system includes:

  • Public website pages
  • Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
  • Service and location information
  • Quote and contact forms
  • Email notifications for customer inquiries
  • Search-engine metadata
  • Analytics integration
  • Website administration for editing content
  • Basic security, privacy, and accessibility features

The following are not included unless added by a change request:

  • Customer accounts or login
  • Online payments
  • Appointment scheduling and calendar integration
  • Live chat
  • Technician dispatching
  • Inventory, invoicing, or accounting
  • Native mobile applications
  • E-commerce

1.5 Assumptions and Constraints

  • The primary target market is Ontario, Canada.
  • Business content, prices, certifications, service areas, and contact details have not yet been confirmed and are marked as TBD.
  • Quote requests do not create a legally binding booking or confirmed appointment.
  • A single public website administrator will be sufficient for initial operations.
  • The website will use HTTPS and will be hosted on a commercially supported web host.
  • No appointment-availability or technician-dispatch integration is required in the initial release.

2. Overall Description

2.1 Product Perspective

The website is a standalone public-facing web application. It will integrate with email delivery and optional analytics services and support a CMS or equivalent content-management mechanism.

2.2 Product Functions

  • Present company and service information
  • Categorize HVAC and appliance services
  • Capture customer quote requests
  • Route customer messages to business staff
  • Display service coverage areas
  • Provide click-to-call and email actions
  • Present verified testimonials
  • Support content updates
  • Produce crawlable, search-friendly pages

2.3 User Classes

UserDescription
VisitorViews information and submits inquiries
AdministratorUpdates pages, services, locations, testimonials, and settings
Support recipientReceives and follows up on submitted inquiries

2.4 Operating Environment

  • Modern desktop and mobile browsers
  • Responsive layouts from 320 pixels wide upward
  • Public hosting with HTTPS
  • Web server, database, and CMS or static-site build system selected during implementation

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints

  • The public interface must be accessible and usable without JavaScript for core content.
  • User data must be processed only for the stated inquiry purpose.
  • Form submissions must be protected against spam and abuse.
  • Content must be maintainable by a non-technical administrator.
  • Third-party scripts must not block meaningful page rendering.

3. Specific Requirements

3.1 Functional Requirements

FR-01: Home Page

The system shall provide a home page containing:

  • Company name and concise value proposition
  • Primary CTA to request a quote
  • Primary CTA to call the business
  • HVAC and appliance service summaries
  • Trust indicators, if supplied and verified
  • Service-area summary
  • Contact information
  • Selected testimonials
  • Prominent emergency or fast-response messaging only if confirmed

FR-02: About Page

The system shall provide an About PowerBreeze page describing purpose, experience, service approach, residential and commercial coverage, customer commitment, and authorized team or technician information.

FR-03: Service Pages

The system shall provide category and detail pages for confirmed HVAC and appliance services.

HVAC: air conditioner repair and installation, furnace repair and installation, heat pump repair and installation, HVAC maintenance, thermostat repair and replacement, ductless mini-split systems, and emergency service if offered.

Appliances: refrigerator, oven, range, stove, dishwasher, washer, and dryer repair, plus appliance installation and maintenance.

Each service detail shall include description, common symptoms, included work, property applicability, repair and installation options, a quote CTA, and approved FAQ content.

FR-04: Location Pages

The system shall provide a GTA and surrounding-area landing page. Individual location pages may be published only where substantively supported. Location content shall include the location name, residential and commercial coverage, available services, local CTA, and accurate non-duplicated copy.

FR-05: Quote Request Form

The form shall collect full name, phone number, email address, service category and type, postal code, property type, preferred contact method and timeframe, appliance or equipment type, issue description, and consent to be contacted. Required fields shall be clearly marked and contact and postal-code formats shall be validated.

FR-06: Quote Submission

The system shall display confirmation, create a backend or CRM import record, send a configured support notification, optionally send a customer confirmation, prevent duplicates, and prevent invalid or incomplete submissions.

FR-07: Contact Page

The contact page shall show the phone number, email address, business hours, service area, contact form, optional map, and a clear response-time expectation.

FR-08: Telephone and Email Actions

The system shall use device-friendly telephone links and valid email links for published contact details.

FR-09: Testimonials

The system shall display only supplied and approved testimonials. Administrators shall be able to add, edit, publish, unpublish, and reorder testimonials.

FR-10: Gallery

The system may display approved work and equipment photos with alternative text. Photos shall not disclose customer property or personal information.

FR-11: Frequently Asked Questions

The system shall provide reusable, accessible FAQ components supporting category and page-level questions.

FR-12: Navigation

The system shall provide header, mobile, and footer navigation, nested-page breadcrumbs, active-page indication, and links to service and contact pages.

FR-13: Search

Site search should be provided when navigation becomes inefficient. Results shall be relevant, exclude unpublished content, and state when no result is found.

FR-14: CMS Administration

Authorized administrators shall create, edit, preview, publish, and archive pages, services, locations, testimonials, and media, and update navigation, contact details, and business hours.

FR-15: SEO and Social Metadata

The system shall support unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService schema, breadcrumb and approved FAQ schema, XML sitemap, robots configuration, and image alt text.

FR-16: Analytics

The system shall track where consent permits: quote-form starts and submissions, telephone and email clicks, and key service-page views. Tracking controls shall be respected.

FR-17: Privacy and Consent

The system shall provide a Privacy Policy, analytics or cookie notice when needed, legal consent controls, and a footer privacy link.

FR-18: Error Pages

The system shall provide branded 404 and generic unavailable pages with navigation and contact options.

3.2 External Interface Requirements

3.2.1 User Interfaces

  • Branding shall use a consistent blue/green palette unless replaced by an approved brand guide.
  • Typography shall remain readable across breakpoints.
  • Links and controls shall include visible keyboard focus.
  • Form validation shall be understandable and accessible.
  • Call buttons shall remain discoverable on mobile devices.
  • The layout shall avoid horizontal scrolling at supported widths.

3.2.2 Hardware Interfaces

No direct hardware interfaces are required.

3.3 Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-01: Performance

Core content shall become visible within two seconds on a representative broadband connection. Largest Contentful Paint shall be below 2.5 seconds, Cumulative Layout Shift below 0.1, and Interaction to Next Paint below 200 milliseconds at the 75th percentile. Images shall be compressed and served in modern formats.

NFR-02: Availability

Public pages shall target 99.9 percent monthly availability, excluding planned maintenance. Hosting shall provide monitored backups and recovery procedures.

NFR-03: Scalability

The system shall support at least 500 concurrent users and expected marketing traffic peaks.

NFR-04: Security

The system shall use HTTPS, strong authenticated administration, authorization controls, login throttling, server-side form validation, protected secrets, dependency scanning, a retention schedule, and private customer submissions.

NFR-05: Accessibility

The website shall conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, support keyboard and assistive technologies, and provide meaningful names, alternatives, contrast, and accessible form errors.

NFR-06: SEO

The system shall use semantic headings, short descriptive URLs, descriptive links, synchronized sitemap updates, and non-duplicated location and service content.

NFR-07: Usability

A visitor shall reach the quote form within two clicks. Contact actions shall remain visible, mobile navigation shall work with one hand, and validation shall be clear and corrective.

NFR-08: Maintainability

The project shall use version control, environment-specific configuration, repeatable deployment, documented administration, and isolated third-party integrations.

NFR-09: Compatibility

The system shall support current and previous major releases of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Android Chrome, and Mobile Safari.

NFR-10: Backup and Recovery

Website data shall be backed up daily and retained according to agreement. Recovery procedures shall be tested, with a critical restore achievable within four hours.

NFR-11: Privacy

The system shall collect only necessary information, keep inquiries private, disclose third-party purposes, and support approved data-removal requests.

5. Acceptance Criteria

  • Home, About, services, locations, contact, privacy, and error pages are complete.
  • Every implemented service and location is supported by confirmed business information.
  • All links and calls to action point to valid destinations.
  • The quote form validates, submits, notifies staff, and shows confirmation.
  • Spam protection is active.
  • Responsive checks pass from 320 to 1920 pixels.
  • Core pages pass WCAG 2.1 Level AA automated and manual checks.
  • Metadata, sitemap, robots, and canonical URLs are configured.
  • Analytics and consent behavior are verified.
  • HTTPS and security headers are active.
  • Production backup and restore testing is complete.
  • Performance targets pass in representative testing.
  • Search, duplicate, broken-link, and spam tests pass.
