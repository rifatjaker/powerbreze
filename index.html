1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the Power Breeze Engineering public website. It reflects the company's electro-mechanical engineering, power infrastructure, electrical contracting, products, and project portfolio as represented on its existing company website.

1.2 Scope

Power Breeze Engineering is an electro-mechanical contractor and power engineering products and services provider, established in 2012. The website will serve government and private-sector clients, procurement teams, project owners, and engineering partners. It will present verified services, imported and supplied products, project experience, client references, credentials, offices, and contact channels.

The in-scope system includes:

  • Public website pages
  • Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
  • Electro-mechanical service, product, and project information
  • Company, credential, client, and office information
  • General, project, and product inquiry forms
  • Email notifications for business inquiries
  • Search-engine metadata
  • Consent-aware analytics integration
  • Website administration for editing content
  • Basic security, privacy, and accessibility features

The following are not included unless added by a change request:

  • Customer accounts or login
  • Online payments
  • Appointment scheduling and calendar integration
  • Live chat
  • Technician dispatching
  • Inventory, invoicing, or accounting
  • Native mobile applications
  • E-commerce

1.5 Assumptions and Constraints

  • Bangladesh is the primary operating market; a Canada office is also listed on the existing company website. Geographic service coverage must be confirmed before location-specific claims are published.
  • The existing company website is the source of the service and product categories. Product availability, technical specifications, licenses, and project/client permissions must be verified before publication.
  • Inquiries do not create a legally binding quotation, contract, purchase order, or confirmed project.
  • A single public website administrator will be sufficient for initial operations.
  • The website will use HTTPS and will be hosted on a commercially supported web host.
  • License, certification, enlistment, project, and client information must be approved and substantiated before publication.

2. Overall Description

2.1 Product Perspective

The website is a standalone public-facing company and capability site. It will integrate with email delivery and optional analytics services and support a CMS or equivalent content-management mechanism for products, services, projects, clients, and corporate information.

2.2 Product Functions

  • Present company profile, verified credentials, and engineering capabilities
  • Categorize electro-mechanical services and products
  • Showcase approved projects and client references
  • Capture project, product, and general business inquiries
  • Present verified office and service-area information
  • Provide click-to-call and email actions
  • Support content updates
  • Produce crawlable, search-friendly pages

2.3 User Classes

UserDescription
Visitor / potential clientReviews company capabilities, products, projects, and submits an inquiry
Procurement representativeReviews product categories and submits a supply or tender-related inquiry
AdministratorUpdates company pages, services, products, projects, clients, media, and settings
Inquiry recipientReceives and follows up on submitted inquiries

2.4 Operating Environment

  • Modern desktop and mobile browsers
  • Responsive layouts from 320 pixels wide upward
  • Public hosting with HTTPS
  • Web server, database, and CMS or static-site build system selected during implementation

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints

  • The public interface must be accessible and usable without JavaScript for core content.
  • User data must be processed only for the stated inquiry purpose.
  • Form submissions must be protected against spam and abuse.
  • Content must be maintainable by a non-technical administrator.
  • Third-party scripts must not block meaningful page rendering.
  • Project details, client identities, and tender content must be approved before public display.

3. Specific Requirements

3.1 Functional Requirements

FR-01: Home Page

The home page shall feature the Power Breeze Engineering name and value proposition, establishment year, government/private-sector focus, a project/product inquiry CTA, engineering capabilities, featured products and approved projects, verified credentials, and confirmed office details.

FR-02: Company Profile

The profile shall describe the company's establishment in 2012, government and private-sector work, mission, vision, strengths, turnkey approach, and approved management/engineering biographies. The existing website claims first-class electro-mechanical contractor status, ABC License, and Electrical Supervisory License; verify the exact issuing authority, current status, and evidence before publication.

FR-03: Engineering Services

The website shall present verified service scope, geographic availability, and capabilities including:

  • 132/33/11 kV substation supply, installation, testing, and commissioning
  • Transformer, HT/LT switchgear, power factor improvement plant, BBT, CT/PT, lightning arrester, DOFC, isolator, and insulator works
  • Diesel/gas generators and synchronization/common-bus panels
  • Solar plants and solar security lighting
  • HT/LT underground and overhead cables, power lines, and commercial power connections
  • Electrical wiring, distribution boards, motor control panels, changeover switches, and industrial/commercial lighting
  • Electrical grounding and earthing, including GI pipe laying and boring where applicable
  • MSPP site survey, network planning, design, and grounding work
  • Battery, rectifier, and OSN installation, commissioning, and maintenance
  • HVAC, elevators, cooling towers, lighting protection, and fire safety systems
  • Turnkey electro-mechanical and civil construction, including interior decoration where offered

Service pages shall describe factual project scope and applicable delivery stages. No unverified technical rating, certification, or service availability may be implied.

FR-04: Products and Equipment

The product catalogue shall present the company's listed import/supply categories, subject to current availability and specification confirmation:

  • 33/11 kV, 2500 A vacuum circuit breaker (VCB); 11 kVA distribution dry-type transformer
  • 132/33/11 kV underground cable and gas-insulated substation (GIS)
  • Lighting protection system (LPS) and fire safety system (FSS)
  • Batteries, battery chargers, oil testing sets, and vacuum oil centrifuging machines
  • Lightning arresters, DOFC, isolators, insulators, CT/PT, pre-paid meters, and optical fiber
  • Solar plants, BBT systems, passenger/bed elevators
  • Transformers, distribution boxes, synchronization panels, solar power systems, and diesel generators

Product records shall support descriptions, approved images, verified specifications, availability notes, and inquiry actions. Online purchase is out of scope.

FR-05: Project Portfolio

Visitors shall be able to browse approved projects by title, client/sector, location, scope, verified capacity/voltage, completion period, and imagery. Confidential project details and client identities shall not be published without authorization.

FR-06: Clients and Enlistments

The site may display government/private-sector clients, procurement enlistments, and institutional references with permission and current verification; their display shall not imply endorsement.

FR-07: Offices and Coverage

The site shall publish confirmed Dhaka head-office, local-office, and Canada-office details where current. Coverage statements shall be confirmed by the company and shall not create unsupported local-service claims.

FR-08: Business Inquiry Form

The form shall support general, project/service, and product/procurement inquiries. As applicable, it shall collect name, organization, role, phone, email, inquiry type, service or product, project location, quantity or technical scope, message, and contact consent. Optional attachments shall be type/size restricted. The form shall discourage submission of confidential tender material.

FR-09: Inquiry Submission

Valid submissions shall receive confirmation, be recorded in the approved backend/CRM queue, notify the configured business inbox, optionally send a customer receipt, prevent duplicates, and reject invalid input.

FR-10: Contact

The contact page shall show confirmed phone, fax, email, office addresses, verified business hours when provided, and a general contact form with optional maps.

FR-11: Telephone and Email Actions

Published phone numbers shall use device-friendly telephone links, and published email addresses shall use valid email links.

FR-12: Clients, Credentials, and Featured Content

Approved client references, license/certificate evidence, project highlights, and management messages may be published. Formal credentials require permission and up-to-date validation.

FR-13: Project and Product Gallery

The gallery shall use approved project/product images with alt text and shall not expose confidential project documents, restricted facilities, or personal information.

FR-14: Frequently Asked Questions

Accessible, approved FAQs may cover procurement, project delivery, products, and services.

FR-15: Navigation

Navigation shall connect company profile, services, products, clients, projects, gallery, and contact content with accessible desktop/mobile menus, breadcrumbs, and active-page indication.

FR-16: Search and Filtering

Site search and project/product filters should exclude unpublished content and provide a clear no-results state.

FR-17: CMS Administration

Authorized administrators shall manage pages, services, products, projects, client/reference records, credentials, offices, media, navigation, contact details, and metadata, with preview and publication controls.

FR-18: SEO and Social Metadata

The system shall support unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, Organization or relevant contracting-business schema, breadcrumbs, approved FAQ schema, XML sitemap, robots configuration, and alt text.

FR-19: Analytics

Where consent permits, aggregate analytics may track inquiry starts/submissions by type, phone/email clicks, and key service, product, and project views. Do not send project/tender inquiry contents to analytics.

FR-20: Privacy and Consent

The system shall provide a Privacy Policy, analytics or cookie notice when needed, legal consent controls, and a footer privacy link.

FR-21: Error Pages

The system shall provide branded 404 and generic unavailable pages with navigation and contact options.

3.3 Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-01: Performance

Core content shall become visible within two seconds on a representative broadband connection. Largest Contentful Paint shall be below 2.5 seconds, Cumulative Layout Shift below 0.1, and Interaction to Next Paint below 200 milliseconds at the 75th percentile. Images shall be compressed and served in modern formats.

NFR-02: Availability

Public pages shall target 99.9 percent monthly availability, excluding planned maintenance. Hosting shall provide monitored backups and recovery procedures.

NFR-03: Scalability

The system shall support at least 500 concurrent users and expected marketing traffic peaks.

NFR-04: Security

The system shall use HTTPS, strong authenticated administration, authorization controls, login throttling, server-side form validation, protected secrets, dependency scanning, a retention schedule, and private business inquiries. Optional project attachments shall be type/size restricted, scanned, and stored outside public executable paths.

NFR-05: Accessibility

The website shall conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, support keyboard and assistive technologies, and provide meaningful names, alternatives, contrast, and accessible form errors.

NFR-06: SEO

The system shall use semantic headings, short descriptive URLs, descriptive links, synchronized sitemap updates, and non-duplicated location and service content.

NFR-07: Usability

A visitor shall reach a project or product inquiry form within two clicks. Contact actions shall remain visible, mobile navigation shall work with one hand, and validation shall be clear and corrective.

NFR-08: Maintainability

The project shall use version control, environment-specific configuration, repeatable deployment, documented administration, and isolated third-party integrations.

NFR-09: Compatibility

The system shall support current and previous major releases of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Android Chrome, and Mobile Safari.

NFR-10: Backup and Recovery

Website data shall be backed up daily and retained according to agreement. Recovery procedures shall be tested, with a critical restore achievable within four hours.

NFR-11: Privacy

The system shall collect only necessary information, keep inquiries private, disclose third-party purposes, and support approved data-removal requests.

5. Acceptance Criteria

  • Home, company profile, services, products, projects, clients/references, contact, privacy, and error pages are complete.
  • The content reflects the company's actual electro-mechanical and power engineering work.
  • Product specifications, credentials, client references, and project details have company approval and evidence.
  • All links and calls to action point to valid destinations.
  • Project, product, and general inquiry forms validate, submit, notify staff, and show confirmation.
  • Spam protection is active.
  • Responsive checks pass from 320 to 1920 pixels.
  • Core pages pass WCAG 2.1 Level AA automated and manual checks.
  • Metadata, sitemap, robots, and canonical URLs are configured.
  • Analytics and consent behavior are verified.
  • HTTPS and security headers are active.
  • Production backup and restore testing is complete.
  • Performance targets pass in representative testing.
  • Search, duplicate, broken-link, and spam tests pass.
